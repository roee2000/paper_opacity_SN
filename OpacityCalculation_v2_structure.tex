\begin{equation}
    \label{eq:kappa_avg}
    \kappa_{\rm avg}(t)
    =
    \frac{\displaystyle\int_{v_{\rm diff}}^{v_{\rm max}}
        \kappa_R^{\rm exp}\,\rho\,t\,dv}
         {\displaystyle\int_{v_{\rm diff}}^{v_{\rm max}}\rho\,t\,dv}
    =
    \frac{\tau(v_{\rm diff})}{\Sigma(v_{\rm diff})}.
\end{equation}

Figure~\ref{fig:all_lanthanides_opacity} shows a general trend across the

from approximately $1$ to $1.5$~eV, although individual elements show exceptions.

This decrease is especially clear in the black curve, which represents a mixture
of all lanthanides with equal atomic number fractions, rather than an arithmetic
average of their individual opacities. We note that the decrease in the opacity appears
mainly for the Rosseland mean and not for the Planck mean.

The HRF--HULLAC comparison for Ho, Er, and Tm in panel (c) partially
